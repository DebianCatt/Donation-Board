! IMPORTANT !
     * Inside the Products.lua (module script)
       are examples of of Developer Products.
       These are the Products that the donators should by as a form of Donation

     * YOU MUST CREATE YOUR OWN Developer Product in your game's settings page,
       copy it's ID and replace the " ProductId = "123456" " on the script, change the
       product price for how much is it
       and start earning robux.

     * You can add as many Products as you want.


Note:
     - Robux earnings are held for 3 days by roblox before giving it to you.
     - this system won't work on roblox studio.
